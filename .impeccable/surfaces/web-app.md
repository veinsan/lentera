# Surface: Lentera app shell, sign-in, and board

Scope: every app route under web/app (sign-in, dashboard board, planner, worklist, student profile, insights, upload, model). Visitor mode: Operate. Audience: DSCO final judges watching a presenter; demo roles coordinator, advisor, admin. Job: see the week 4 finding, see how advisor capacity turns into students reached, open one student and act. Constraints: Indonesian UI copy, every number from an artifact or query, one-click sign-in, projector readability.

## Direction contract

THESIS: This week's contact list is a pinned noticeboard. Each flagged student is a sheet with a tier pin; each advisor owns as many pin slots as their hours allow. Refuses the category default of KPI tiles over a chart grid.

OWN-WORLD: Warm putty ground #ECE7DD, paper sheets #FBFAF6 with soft offset shadow and 20-24px radius, near-black ink #16140F, slate rail and contrast panels #1F2B2E, one lime highlighter #D9F24A used as a marker stroke behind key numbers and as the pin of the selected item. Tier ramp kept from the validated palette, shown as round pins with labels. Archivo (condensed for numerals and headlines), Geist for UI, Geist Mono for data. Four type sizes; rank by weight and case.

STORY: The judge reads one sentence with two highlighted numbers, sees five advisors' columns fill with real students, drags capacity and watches the board refill, then opens a student to see why and what to do.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slate rail left (wordmark, six nav items, role panel with one-click role switch). Main: top row, a pinned paper sheet with the headline finding (3,875 flagged; 353 reached) and four facts, beside a slate capacity card with advisor and hours steppers and the +59 marginal reach in lime. Below, the board: one column per advisor with student sheets (pin colour = tier, ID, module, probability, action), open slots as dashed pin holes, queue count in the header.

FORM: Assigned direction 6 of 7 (campus noticeboard), seed:1359da91bdeff352. Raises: type scale locked to four sizes (timetable rack); capacity never overflows, the queue sits below the line (cassette j-card); weekly small multiples share one scale (botanical folio); one control remaps the whole board live (variable font specimen); one figure owns each screen (catalog sleeve).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
